Featured Excursions

Chilko River Challenge

Experience North America’s longest stretch of continuous Class IV whitewater! This thrilling multi-day trip takes you through turquoise waters surrounded by alpine wilderness and stunning canyon vistas.

  • Duration: 3 Days
  • Min Age: 16+
  • Includes: Meals, Gear & Camping Equipment

Iowa Strait Family Drift

Perfect for families with young children or first-time rafters seeking a peaceful day on the river. Enjoy scenic mountain views, spot local wildlife, and pause for a relaxing beach lunch stop along the shores.

  • Duration: Half Day (4 hrs)
  • Min Age: 6+
  • Includes: Safety Gear & Lunch

River Benue Express

Our most popular one-day excursion features non-stop action through rolling rapids, wave trains, and power drops. Ideal for corporate team-building outings, friend groups, and intermediate paddlers.

  • Duration: Full Day (7 hrs)
  • Min Age: 12+
  • Includes: Full Gear, Guide & Lunch Service
